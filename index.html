Each guide contains an educational schematic, aim, apparatus, procedure, observation, result framework, precautions and viva prompts. These are study guides; the teacher/laboratory manual remains the authority for actual practical work.

Experiment 1: Vernier Calipers Physics

Aim: Measure the diameter or dimensions of a small object.

Apparatus: Scale with main scale + sliding vernier + jaws.

Procedure: Read main scale, find vernier coincidence, apply least count and zero correction.

Observation: Record multiple readings and mean them.

Calculation/Interpretation: Write the measured quantities with units, substitute values into the relevant relation, and show the final result with appropriate significant figures.

Result: State the measured quantity or qualitative conclusion clearly.

Precautions: Follow the school laboratory manual and teacher supervision; use clean apparatus, avoid parallax, record units, repeat readings and never improvise procedures.

Viva questions: What is the principle? Why is this apparatus used? What is the main source of error? What happens if a key condition is changed?
